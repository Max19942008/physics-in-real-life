Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Summary

Physics Uploader is a personal tool that posts the channel owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts. It does not collect, sell or share personal data about anyone else.

2. What the app stores

  • Access tokens that the platform (for example TikTok) issues after the owner logs in and approves the app. They are kept only in a private settings file on the owner's computer and are used only to upload videos to the owner's account.
  • The owner's account ID on the platform, needed to post to the right account.
  • A local list of posted videos, so the same video is not posted twice.

3. What the app does not do

  • It does not read other users' profiles, videos, comments, messages or followers.
  • It does not run ads, tracking or analytics.
  • It does not send data to any server other than the official platform APIs it posts to.

4. TikTok data

The app asks TikTok only for user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish. They are used to identify the owner's account and to upload and publish the owner's videos. Nothing from TikTok is shared with anyone.

5. Deleting data

The owner can remove the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. Deleting the local settings file removes all stored tokens.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from viewers of any age.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Questions: send a message to the Physics in Real Life channel on TikTok, Instagram or Facebook.
